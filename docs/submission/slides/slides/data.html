All on one 2.5° grid in monthly steps, January 2001 to December 2025. A cell is tested when at least 90% of its months hold a value. About 100 GB of NASA files were read to make it.

The first scan on Saturday had the first four plus earthquakes. Snow cover and the two ocean variables were added overnight under the same rules, and we say so: they were not in the scan as first declared. Vegetation was also read again overnight, keeping only the pixels MODIS marks as good or marginal, which cost about a third of its testable cells, mostly where winters are snowy. No second product is fully independent of the first, and each finding says what the two have in common.
