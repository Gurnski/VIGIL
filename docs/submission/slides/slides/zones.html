Zones

Draw any region and get its trends

Draw an outline on the globe with the pen. The zone is the grid cells whose centres it holds, and the app draws their stepped edge so you see exactly what the numbers cover.

The service averages those cells by area and runs the scan’s own test on the result, one variable at a time.

A zone chosen by eye is not a planned test. Every zone result says so, and none is ever called significant after correction.

In the demo, draw one live: it takes about two seconds to come back. The panel lists the trend per decade with its 95% interval for every variable, and says which variables could not be tested there and why. Snow cover over the Amazon carries the service’s own warning, “Snow is rare here”: a trend in almost nothing is not a finding. Zones are saved on the machine and can be deleted.
