Seeing it

The size of a change, and the year it arrived

Relief

Each cell rises from the globe by the size of its trend. Colour still says which way. A region changing fast stands out before anyone reads a legend.

Years

Press play and the layer runs from 2001 to 2025, each cell against its own normal. The Amazon’s dry years arrive on screen as they happened.

The years are for looking. No test is run on them and no finding rests on them.

The picture is precipitation over the Amazon in 2023, against each cell’s own normal: blue is drier, and height is how far from normal. Sea temperature over the North Pacific either side of 2013 is the other one worth playing. Say plainly that a tall column is a large change, not a sure one: “Significant only” is the switch for sureness.
