Limits

What VIGIL does not claim

A link is not a cause

Cascades are lagged correlations inside one cell. They are reported as associations and never as one thing causing another.

Significant is not large

Every finding shows the size of the change and its interval beside its p-value, before and after correction.

Re-tested is not proven

A second product shares inputs with the first. Each re-tested finding says what the two have in common.

A cell is not a place

A 2.5° cell averages a lot of ground. A finding describes the cell, not a valley or a city inside it.

Every finding carries its own limitations, written by the service, and the app shows all of them. Earthquake findings in particular: the catalogue grows more complete with time, so a rising count can be better detection.
