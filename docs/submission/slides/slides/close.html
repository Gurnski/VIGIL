VIGIL

Discovery first. Evidence for every finding.

See the scan in a browser at keepvigil.earth, or run the desktop app from the repository at github.com/Gurnski/VIGIL.

Data: NASA MERRA-2, GPM IMERG, GLDAS, MODIS Terra and Aqua, GISTEMP and GPCP. Earthquakes: USGS. Imagery: NASA Blue Marble and Black Marble.

Close by repeating the one idea: nothing in the demo was chosen in advance, and every number on screen can be traced to its source, its method and its limits.
