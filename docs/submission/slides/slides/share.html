Beyond one screen

Take a room with you, and hand the results on

Present

Their screens follow yours

The service hands the viewer to any phone on the network. Choose a finding, a layer or a zone and it appears on theirs.

Explore

Or they go their own way

Anyone can stop following and look round alone, with the same findings, evidence and Detective.

Export

CSV and GeoJSON

Every finding with its cell as a polygon, its interval, its p-values and its re-test outcome. Valid GeoJSON, made for QGIS.

Look

NASA Worldview

Each finding links to its own cell in Worldview, to see the place itself in satellite imagery.

The public site can run with no service: every finding’s chart is a file beside the page.

To use this live: start the service with --host 0.0.0.0, press Present, and read out the address on the card. Only the presenter’s machine can change what is on show. If the venue’s Wi-Fi keeps devices apart, skip it and say the site at keepvigil.earth shows the same scan.
