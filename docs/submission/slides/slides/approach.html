From NASA’s archive to a ranked finding

1

One grid

Every product is averaged onto the same 2.5° cells and monthly steps, 2001 to 2025.

2

Test every cell

Mann-Kendall with Sen’s slope on anomalies, corrected for autocorrelation.

3

Correct for many tests

False discovery rate control across each variable’s tests. Both p-values are kept.

4

Look across systems

Change points, lagged links between variables, and neighbours trending opposite ways.

5

Rank in the open

A score anyone can recompute: effect size, certainty, coverage, robustness, links.

6

Test again

Where a second product exists, each trend is tested on it too. Only a confirmed one is called re-tested.

Every setting is fixed before the scan and written into the run record: the grid, the period, the 90% coverage rule, the 0.05 threshold. Cells that are skipped are recorded with the reason. No setting of the test was changed after results were seen. Three things about the data were, and we say so: three variables were added overnight, vegetation was read again keeping only good-quality pixels, and the second record for sea temperature was chosen after its first results.
