6,122

findings ranked: trends, change points, lagged links and contradictions

Another 63,943 tests were skipped, and each one says why: sea or ice under a land variable, or fewer than 90% of months with data. Besides the 5,450 trends there are 346 lagged links between variables, 301 change points and 25 pairs of neighbours trending opposite ways.

These are read straight from the run record of the scan frozen on Sunday morning. The scan itself takes about three minutes on a desktop PC once the data is on the grid. Skipped tests matter as much as run ones: the app can say of any cell why it has no finding.
