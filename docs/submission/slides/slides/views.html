Ways to look

One layer on a globe, in relief, or as a flat map

Relief and tilt

Switch Relief on and each cell stands up by the size of its trend. The view leans over with it, so a column is seen from its side.

Flat map

In the web viewer the globe unrolls into a map of the whole world. The layer, the columns, the markers and the zones go with it.

Neither changes a value, a colour or a finding.

The clip plays by itself and loops. It is the web viewer at keepvigil.earth on the real scan: precipitation over the Amazon. Relief stands the columns up and leans the view over, Flat map unrolls the globe and rolls it up again, and the place search takes it back to the Amazon. The flat map is in the web viewer only. The desktop app has relief and tilt. The satellites are switched off in the clip because they are not drawn on the flat map.
