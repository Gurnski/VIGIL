NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Earth System Trend Detective

VIGIL

An Earth system trend detective that finds what is changing, tests it again, and shows its evidence.

[Team names], University of Plymouth, 14 to 15 November 2026

VIGIL stands for Visualising and Identifying Global Indicators of Long-term change. One line to open: we did not pick a famous event and build a demo round it. We scanned the whole planet the same way and let the findings rank themselves. The Earth on this slide is the app's own globe, drawn from NASA Blue Marble and Black Marble and lit as on a June evening.
