Greening across central China

The top five findings are all vegetation rising, from the Loess Plateau south to Chongqing, each confirmed on Aqua’s record.

The southern Amazon

In one cell, rainfall falling by 32 mm a month per decade, vegetation and soil moisture falling, and the air warming. All four confirmed on a second product.

And a caution

Soil moisture ranks high too, and it is the variable we trust least.

China’s greening from replanting and farming is well documented, including by NASA from this same MODIS record. We did not tell VIGIL to look there.
